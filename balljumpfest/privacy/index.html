The short version

Ball Jump Fest is a game that runs entirely on your device.

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not know who you are.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. We do not operate a server that receives data from the app.

5.1 Motion and sensor data

The App uses your device's accelerometer, through Apple's Core Motion framework, to read how far you are tilting the device so it can move the ball left and right.

  • The reading is used in the moment, in memory, within the game loop, to compute the ball's horizontal velocity.
  • It is never written to storage, never logged, and never transmitted.
  • It stops as soon as you leave the game screen.
  • On current iOS versions, accelerometer access of this kind does not require a permission prompt. It cannot be used to identify you, and we make no attempt to do so.

5.2 Audio and haptics

Game sounds are played from audio files bundled inside the App. The App uses the "ambient" audio mode, meaning it mixes with whatever you were already listening to rather than interrupting it, and stays silent when your device's ring/silent switch is set to silent. The App does not access your microphone and does not record any audio. Haptic feedback is generated locally by the device.

14. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, as required by Article 32 GDPR and comparable laws.

In addition:

  • Data stored by the App sits in the App's private container, protected by the operating system's sandbox and by device-level file encryption when your device is locked with a passcode.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock and to keep iOS up to date.
